FAC READINESS PORTAL — FIRESTORE STAGE 1 SETUP

1. FIRESTORE SECURITY RULES
   - Open Firebase Console > Firestore Database > Rules.
   - Replace the existing rules with all content from firestore.rules.
   - Click Publish.

2. USER PROFILE REQUIREMENT
   Every Firebase Authentication account must have a matching document:

   Collection : users
   Document ID: the user's Authentication UID

   Required fields:
   - displayName (string)
   - email       (string)
   - role        (string: superadmin, admin, or viewer)
   - station     (string, uppercase: JKT, DPS, SUB, KNO, etc.)
   - active      (boolean: true)

3. ACCESS SCOPE
   - superadmin: network access and Portal Management.
   - JKT station: network-wide operational data access.
   - other stations: only records from the same station.
   - admin: may add, edit, import, and delete operational records.
   - viewer: read-only access.

4. COLLECTIONS CREATED AUTOMATICALLY
   The website creates these collections when the first record is saved:
   - facMembers
   - equipment
   - trainings
   - checklistTemplates
   - checklistRecords
   - settings
   - auditLogs

5. NETLIFY
   Upload the ZIP once to the existing Netlify site. Changes to records, roles,
   and stations are then handled in Firebase and do not require another deploy.

6. STAGE 1 LIMITATION
   File bytes such as PDF, images, and Excel attachments are not uploaded in
   this stage. Their online upload will be handled later with Firebase Storage.
